Personal Summary

Ph.D. in Advanced Mechanical Engineering and Robotics from Ritsumeikan University, Japan.

MMy research focuses on bio-inspired snake-like robots with compliant passive joints, covering mechanical design, dynamic modeling, and locomotion control. I have experience in robotic prototyping, MATLAB simulation, SolidWorks design, and experimental validation.
